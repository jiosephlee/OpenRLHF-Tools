CYP2D6 is a cytochrome P450 enzyme expressed primarily in the liver and also in the CNS (substantia nigra, cerebellum). Despite constituting only about 2-4% of hepatic CYP content, it metabolizes approximately 25% of clinically used drugs, including many CNS-active agents (antidepressants, antipsychotics, opioids), beta-blockers, and antiarrhythmics. CYP2D6 is highly polymorphic — poor metabolizers (PMs, ~5-10% of Caucasians) lack functional enzyme, while ultra-rapid metabolizers carry gene duplications, creating large inter-individual variability in drug exposure.

The CYP2D6 active site is a relatively small, elongated cavity with a key negatively charged residue (Asp301) at the entrance that forms an ion pair or hydrogen bond with basic nitrogen atoms in substrates. The heme iron is at the base. This acidic residue is the defining selectivity feature — it creates a strong preference for basic, cationic substrates.

Molecular features strongly associated with being a CYP2D6 substrate: the presence of a basic nitrogen atom (pKa > 7, typically a secondary or tertiary amine) that is protonated at physiological pH and can form an ion pair with Asp301 — this is the single most important feature. The basic nitrogen is typically located 5-7 Å from the site of oxidation. A planar hydrophobic region (usually an aromatic ring) adjacent to the metabolic site that fits into the hydrophobic pocket of the active site. Many CYP2D6 substrates share a common pharmacophoric pattern: basic N—(2-3 carbon linker)—aromatic ring with the oxidation occurring on or near the aromatic ring. Moderate lipophilicity (logP 1-4). Molecular weight 200-500 Da.

Features arguing against CYP2D6 metabolism: absence of a basic nitrogen — acidic, neutral, or non-ionizable compounds are generally not CYP2D6 substrates. Very large, bulky molecules (MW > 500) cannot fit the small active site. Compounds with no aromatic ring or the aromatic ring too far from the basic nitrogen. Quaternary amines (permanently charged, too bulky for the active site). Very hydrophilic molecules (logP < 0) that cannot partition into the lipophilic active site cavity.
