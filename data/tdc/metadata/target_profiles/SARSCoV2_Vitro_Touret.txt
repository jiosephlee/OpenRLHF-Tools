This dataset measures in vitro antiviral activity against SARS-CoV-2 in cell-based infection assays, as reported by Touret et al. Unlike the Diamond crystallographic fragment screen (which directly measures binding to the 3CL protease), this is a phenotypic assay — compounds are tested for their ability to inhibit viral replication in Vero E6 cells. Active compounds could work through any mechanism: inhibiting viral entry (blocking ACE2-spike interaction or TMPRSS2-mediated priming), blocking viral polymerase (RdRp), inhibiting the 3CL or PL proteases, disrupting host factors required for replication, or interfering with viral assembly and release. The assay measures cytopathic effect (CPE) reduction, so cytotoxic compounds that kill host cells will appear inactive or be excluded.

Because this is a whole-cell phenotypic assay rather than a target-specific biochemical assay, the molecular features that drive activity are more diverse. Active compounds in this screen include repurposed drugs (e.g., remdesivir — an RdRp inhibitor, hydroxychloroquine — a lysosomotropic agent, lopinavir — an HIV protease inhibitor) as well as novel hits from diverse chemical libraries. The hit rate is low — most compounds in a typical screening library show no antiviral activity.

Features that may favor antiviral activity: drug-like physicochemical properties (MW 300-600, moderate logP, good cell permeability) that allow the compound to reach intracellular targets. Functional groups associated with known antiviral mechanisms — nucleoside/nucleotide analogues for RdRp inhibition, peptidomimetic groups for protease inhibition, cationic amphiphilic structures for lysosomotropic accumulation. Adequate aqueous solubility for testing at effective concentrations.

Features arguing against activity: very large, poorly cell-permeable molecules (MW > 800, TPSA > 150 Å²) that cannot reach intracellular targets. Highly lipophilic compounds (logP > 6) with poor solubility. Reactive or cytotoxic compounds — the CPE-reduction assay requires that the compound be non-toxic to host cells at the active concentration. Very hydrophilic molecules (logP < -1) that cannot cross cell membranes.
